# Northstar Digital - Company Profile

## Overview
Northstar Digital is a B2B digital consultancy specializing in digital transformation, CRM integration, workflow automation, and custom Web analytics for mid-market service businesses (50-300 employees).

## Core Philosophy
We focus on pragmatism, fast time-to-value, and reliable implementation. We partner closely with client leadership to replace fragmented legacy processes with streamlined digital infrastructure.

## Key Capabilities
- Website Redesign & Modernization
- CRM Integration & Workflow Automation
- Lead Capture & Lead Nurture Automation
- Web Analytics & Business Intelligence Implementation

## Engagement Model
- Project-based fixed scope engagements
- Advisory and retainer support after project completion
- Dedicated project leads with weekly stakeholder alignment calls
