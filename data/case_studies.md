# Northstar Digital - Case Studies

## Case Study 1: Apex Logistics Consulting
- **Client**: Apex Logistics (120 employees, B2B Consulting)
- **Challenge**: Manual lead capture and slow follow-up times after prospect inquiry calls led to high drop-off rates.
- **Services Provided**: Lead Capture Automation + CRM Integration (HubSpot)
- **Results**:
  - Reduced lead response time from 24 hours to under 15 minutes.
  - Increased conversion from qualified inquiry to proposal by 35%.
  - Saved 12 hours/week per sales rep on manual data entry.

## Case Study 2: Meridian Wealth Advisory
- **Client**: Meridian Wealth (80 employees, Financial Services)
- **Challenge**: Outdated corporate website failing to attract modern clients, with zero tracking on client acquisition sources.
- **Services Provided**: Website Redesign + Web Analytics Implementation
- **Results**:
  - 45% increase in organic traffic within 90 days.
  - Full visibility into marketing channel ROI through GA4 & Looker Studio dashboard.
